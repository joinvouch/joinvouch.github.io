How we use your details

We keep your first name, email, region and, if you add it, your Instagram or phone number. We use them only to invite you to join Vouch when we open, and never sell or share them.

They’re stored securely in the UK by our database provider. We keep them for up to 12 months after we invite you, or until you ask us to delete them, whichever comes first. To see or delete your details, reply to any email we send you, or message us on Instagram or TikTok.

To stop scripts filling the list, we keep a scrambled code for your internet connection for one day, then delete it.
